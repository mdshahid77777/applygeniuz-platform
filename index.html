Common Pitfalls

Why Resumes Fail Screening

Many resumes do not make it past screening filters due to simple structural issues or layout choices.

Lack of Measured Impact

Resumes listing lists of duties without specifying quantitative achievements or numbers. Focus on metrics, scale, and performance results.

Weak Synonym Matching

Checkers looking for exact words, failing if you describe the same concepts differently. ApplyGeniuz matches skills to job requirements semantically.

Parsing Errors

Complex visual templates, charts, or columns that database parsers fail to read. Keep the layouts minimal, readable, and clean.
